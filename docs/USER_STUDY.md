# Local usability study / 本地可用性测试

This is a plan, not a report of completed user research. Use a public or otherwise shareable video when other participants are involved; private lecture recordings, subtitles, questions, notes and results must stay on their owner's computer. Do not use restricted lectures for a shared study.

这是一份测试计划，不代表已经完成用户研究。邀请他人参与时，仅使用公开或明确允许分享的视频；受限制的课程录像、字幕、问题、笔记和结果须留在所有者的电脑上，不得作为共享测试素材。

## Five tasks / 五项任务

Give each participant a suitable video and a question whose relevant moment has been checked in advance. Explain the goal, but do not show the participant which controls to use. / 为参与者准备可分享的视频和事先核对过答案位置的问题，只解释目标，不提前示范操作。

1. Add or select the video and determine whether it is ready to search. / 添加或选择视频，确认它是否可以搜索。
2. Search for the topic and open a result at its timestamp. / 搜索主题，点击结果并跳转到相应时间。
3. Check the video against the returned excerpt and decide whether the evidence supports the result. / 对照原视频和字幕，判断证据是否支持结果。
4. Save a useful moment, add a note, save it, and find it again after refreshing the page. / 收藏有用片段，保存备注，刷新后重新找到它。
5. Leave feedback on the result or evidence and locate the feedback record. / 对结果或证据提交反馈，并找到反馈记录。

For each task record whether the participant completed it without assistance, completed it with assistance, or could not complete it; note the elapsed time and one observed obstacle. These are usability observations, not model accuracy, precision, recall or F1. Record only what participants agree to share; avoid names, private recordings and course text. Keep raw notes local. / 对每项任务记录独立完成、协助完成或未完成，以及耗时和一个观察到的障碍。这些是可用性观察，不是模型准确率、Precision、Recall 或 F1。只记录参与者同意分享的信息，避免姓名、私人录像和课程内容；原始记录保存在本机。

## Suggested record / 记录模板

| Participant code | Task | Outcome | Time | Obstacle / improvement idea |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P01 | 1–5 | Independent / assisted / not completed | Seconds | Observation, without private content |

Summarize the number of participants and tasks completed in each category, the most common obstacle, and the change made in response. Do not present feedback as representative of all students or as evidence of answer correctness. / 汇总参与人数、各类任务完成次数、最常见障碍及后续改进；不要把反馈当成全体学生的代表，也不要作为回答正确率的证据。
